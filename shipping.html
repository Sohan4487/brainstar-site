Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Digital service, no shipping

BrainStar Pro is a digital service delivered inside the BrainStar Android app. Nothing is shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

When you get it

Pro is unlocked on your BrainStar account instantly after your payment is confirmed by our payment partner, usually within a few seconds. It works on any phone where you sign in with the same Google account.

If Pro doesn't unlock

Open the paywall and tap Restore. If it still isn't active within 24 hours of a successful payment, e-mail lens.sohan@gmail.com and we will fix it or refund you (see our Refund Policy).
