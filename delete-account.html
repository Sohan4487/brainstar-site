Delete your BrainStar account

How to ask for deletion

Send an e-mail to lens.sohan@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Delete my BrainStar account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account ID, name, e-mail address and profile picture link.
  • Your Pro status and subscription records.

What we keep

Payment records that Indian tax and accounting law requires us to keep (amount, date and payment reference, with no card or UPI details) are kept for up to 8 years and then deleted.

Data on your phone

Your reel history never leaves your phone. To remove it, uninstall BrainStar or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → BrainStar → Storage.

Cancel Pro first

Deleting your account stops Pro. To stop future charges, also cancel the BrainStar mandate in your UPI app or bank (for example, UPI AutoPay mandates).
