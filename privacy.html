Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

BrainStar ("we", "the app") helps you watch fewer short videos. This policy explains what the app uses, what stays on your phone, and what reaches our server.

1. Accessibility service

To count reels, BrainStar uses Android's Accessibility service. It is used only to:

  • detect when Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels or Snapchat Spotlight is open on screen,
  • count each reel you swipe to,
  • show the counter bubble and check-in popups, and take you out of reels when your limit is reached.

It does not read your messages, passwords, payment details, contacts or what is inside the videos, and it does not record your screen. You turn it on yourself and can turn it off at any time in Android Settings.

2. Data that stays on your phone

The time of each reel, which app it was in, your time spent on reels, your daily limit and your settings. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to our server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Data we keep on our server (only if you sign in)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive from Google your name, e-mail address and profile picture link, and create an account ID. If you buy BrainStar Pro, we also keep your plan, its status and renewal date, and the payment reference from our payment partner. We use this only to keep your Pro working on any phone and to give you support.

We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Payments are handled by Cashfree Payments.

4. Service providers

  • Supabase stores account and subscription data (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • Google provides Sign in with Google.
  • Cashfree Payments processes payments and subscriptions.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, BrainStar sends reminders about your daily limit. They are created on your phone.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and all server data at any time. See Delete your account. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

7. Children

BrainStar is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). On our server, each account can only read its own records.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
