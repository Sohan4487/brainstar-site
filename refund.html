Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What you buy

BrainStar Pro is a digital subscription that unlocks extra features inside the BrainStar Android app (Reels Lock, weekly progress, friend battles). There is no physical product.

Cancellation

  • One-time plans (1 year or 1 month, paid once) do not renew, so there is nothing to cancel. Pro simply ends on its end date.
  • Auto-renewing plans (when offered, e.g. UPI AutoPay) can be cancelled any time from your UPI app or bank mandate settings, or by e-mailing us. Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

Refunds

  • If you were charged twice, charged by mistake, or Pro did not unlock after a successful payment, write to us within 7 days of the payment and we will refund the full amount.
  • If you are not happy with Pro, write to us within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it, no questions asked.
  • After 7 days, payments are not refundable, but you keep Pro until the end of your plan.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the same payment method (UPI, card or bank account) through our payment partner Cashfree Payments, usually within 5–7 working days of approval.

How to ask

E-mail lens.sohan@gmail.com with the Google account e-mail you use in BrainStar and the payment date. See Contact us for other ways to reach us.
